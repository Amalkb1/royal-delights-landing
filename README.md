# Royal Delights Landing

"Build a premium, luxury-feel landing page for 'Mr Delights' — a premium dry fruits and seeds brand.

Brand identity: deep dark green (#0a2e1a or similar) as the primary background color, with rich gold (#d4af37 / gold gradient) as the accent color for text, borders, and icons. The logo features a crown, laurel wreath, and elegant serif + script typography — reflect that same royal, premium aesthetic throughout the site.

Sections needed:

Hero section — dark green background, gold 'Mr Delights' logo/wordmark centered, tagline 'Premium Dry Fruits & Seeds', a short compelling headline about quality and purity, and a prominent gold CTA button ('Shop Now' or 'Explore Collection').

Featured Products — a grid/carousel of product cards (almonds, cashews, pistachios, mixed seeds, dates, etc.) with product images, names, prices, and an 'Add to Cart' button. Cards should have a subtle gold border/hover glow on dark background.

Brand Story section — short paragraph about premium quality sourcing, with an elegant image or icon (wheat/laurel motif) alongside the text.

Why Choose Us — 3-4 feature icons/cards (e.g. '100% Natural', 'Premium Quality', 'Hygienically Packed', 'Fast Delivery') in gold icons on dark cards.

Testimonials section — simple carousel with customer reviews, gold star ratings.

Footer — dark green background, gold text, contact info, social icons, and a newsletter signup input with a gold submit button.

Design style: elegant, minimal, luxury e-commerce feel (like a premium chocolate or jewelry brand), generous white/negative space using the dark green as base, serif fonts for headings, clean sans-serif for body text, smooth hover animations and subtle gold shimmer effects on buttons.

Make it fully responsive for mobile and desktop. Use placeholder product images and text where real content isn't provided."
